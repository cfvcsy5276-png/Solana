About PizzaCoin

PizzaCoin is a community-driven meme token deployed on the Solana blockchain, established to honor the historic Bitcoin pizza transaction. That transaction represents the earliest real-world application of cryptocurrency and has become an irreplaceable cultural symbol.

Unlike traditional meme coins, PizzaCoin focuses on ecosystem building and long-term development. The official website is fully launched, and a dedicated mobile application is in development to provide users with diversified one-stop services.

We adhere to transparency, security, and a community-first approach, locking most of the liquidity to ensure asset safety. PizzaCoin aims to become a valuable and influential token within the Solana ecosystem.

🍕 PizzaCoin Whitepaper v1.0

Honoring the pizza that changed everything. On May 22, 2010, Laszlo Hanyecz paid 10,000 Bitcoin for two pizzas. That day marked the first time crypto touched the real world, and it changed everything. PizzaCoin is built to honor that moment — not as a joke, not as a short-term pump, but as a real movement on Solana to bring crypto back to everyday spending.

PizzaCoin is not a one-person project. It is built by over one hundred communities across crypto, all coming together under one slice. No VCs, no presale, no hidden team allocation. Just people who believe that a meme coin can actually be useful if you give it a real reason to exist. And what better reason than pizza?

The total supply is exactly 1,000,000,000 tokens — one billion, no more, no less. The contract is renounced, meaning nobody can mint new tokens or freeze wallets ever again. The liquidity pool on Raydium is locked for five years, and proof will be posted publicly. There is zero tax on transactions because PizzaCoin is meant to be spent, not hoarded.

70% of the total supply goes straight to the liquidity pool. 15% is reserved for a community airdrop across 100 partner communities, unlocked from day one. Another 10% is set aside for marketing and getting PizzaCoin onto major exchanges, held in a multi-signature wallet controlled by five different community members. The remaining 5% is saved for future events like burns and staking, also behind a multi-sig.

Deflationary plan is simple and symbolic: On every May 22nd (Pizza Day), one ten-millionth of the supply will be burned — one for each Bitcoin that bought those two pizzas. Additional burns will occur when the community hits major milestones (e.g., 100,000 holders or a top-tier exchange listing). The community will also vote quarterly on whether to burn leftover marketing funds. The goal is to remove at least 30% of the total supply within three years.

The roadmap is built around real events, not empty promises. First comes the fair launch on Raydium with no presale and no early access. Then the airdrop to 100 communities and the opening of all social channels. Within the first six months, the focus is on centralized exchange listings, the first Pizza Day burn, and building partnerships with real-world food vendors through Solana Pay. The long-term vision is simple: a coin you can actually use to buy a slice, whether online or in a shop.

The team is anonymous but accountable. All important wallets are multi-signature, and no single person has control. The contract address will be posted 30 minutes before launch on Twitter and Telegram, and everything will be verified on RugCheck and Solscan from the very first block.
