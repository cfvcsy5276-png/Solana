About PizzaCoin

PizzaCoin is a community-driven meme token deployed on the Solana blockchain, established to honor the historic Bitcoin pizza transaction. This transaction represents the earliest practical application of cryptocurrency and has become an irreplaceable cultural symbol.

Unlike traditional meme coins, PizzaCoin focuses on ecological construction and long-term development. Our official website has been fully launched, and a dedicated mobile application is under development to provide users with diversified one-stop services.

We adhere to transparency, security, and community orientation, locking most liquidity to ensure asset safety. PizzaCoin aims to become a valuable and influential token in the Solana ecosystem.
